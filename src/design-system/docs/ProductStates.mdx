# The 55 product states

Each story renders the production screen with deterministic fixtures (baseline `2026-09-04T12:00:00Z`) and is captured at 393 × 852. The design ledger — inventory columns, transition matrix, reference decisions, content model, asset register and decisions — is `docs/design/hifi-decisions.md`; this page is the Storybook-side index, not a second specification.

**Verification mode.** *runtime + story*: the state occurs in the real app flow and is also frozen here. *story*: a passive or hard-to-trigger state that the prototype cannot reach honestly (no camera, no failing catalogue recipe, no scriptable keyboard, no simulator controls in the product — ledger D-24), reproduced deterministically from production code.
