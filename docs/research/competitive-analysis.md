# Competitive Analysis

## Objective

Identify established patterns, differences, and product opportunities directly relevant to calorie calculation and recipe discovery.

## Products Analyzed

- MyFitnessPal
- YAZIO
- Lifesum
- Cal AI
- FatSecret
- Lose It!

## Evaluation Areas

The products were compared across:

- food-input mechanisms;
- calorie calculation;
- portion and serving handling;
- nutrition-information hierarchy;
- repeated logging shortcuts;
- recipe discovery;
- recipe search and filtering;
- recipe-detail information;
- relevant AI-assisted functionality;
- correction and user-control mechanisms.

## Product-Level Observations

### MyFitnessPal

Database- and diary-oriented tracking experience with multiple food-input methods and established nutrition-tracking functionality.

Relevant patterns:
- food search and database lookup;
- barcode and assisted input methods;
- structured nutrition information;
- recipe-related functionality.

### YAZIO

Combines calorie tracking with a comparatively developed recipe-discovery experience.

Relevant patterns:
- multiple food-input mechanisms;
- AI-assisted photo input;
- recipe search and filtering;
- dietary and preference-based recipe discovery;
- explicit review of AI-generated results.

### Lifesum

Combines nutrition tracking with broader healthy-eating guidance and personalized recipe content.

Relevant patterns:
- multiple logging methods;
- nutrition-focused feedback;
- recipe personalization;
- reusable/frequent food shortcuts.

### Cal AI

Uses a strongly photo-first positioning for calorie estimation.

Relevant patterns:
- camera-first calorie input;
- alternative search, barcode, and text input;
- AI-estimated nutrition;
- custom recipe functionality.

### FatSecret

Database- and diary-oriented product with several mechanisms for reducing food-entry effort.

Relevant patterns:
- food database search;
- barcode scanning;
- photo-assisted input;
- recently/frequently eaten foods;
- recipe functionality.

### Lose It!

Diary-led calorie tracking with multiple mechanisms for entering and reusing food data.

Relevant patterns:
- food search;
- barcode scanning;
- photo-assisted input;
- voice input;
- recent foods and meals;
- reusable recipe functionality.
